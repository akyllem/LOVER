Rooms.

Talasea has six room categories spread across the main house, the garden, and the cliff. Every room faces the sea. None are identical. Each is named, not numbered.

The grounds

Things to do, in order of stillness.

Three saltwater pools

Cut into the cliff, refilled by the sea twice daily, no chlorine, no bracelets, no schedule.

The kitchen garden

Two acres of vegetables, herbs, and a small vineyard. Walk it before lunch with the chef if you'd like.

Stargazer rooftop

Open after dinner. Two lounge beds, a bottle, and a sky black enough to see the Milky Way without trying.

Massage by appointment

Maria comes up from the village three days a week. Olive-oil massage in the cliff cabana.
